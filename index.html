Our Story

Where Gurukul
Meets Montessori

Little Gurukul Preschool is Kundli's most trusted Montessori environment — blending the time-honored Gurukul principles of respect, curiosity, and discipline with the internationally acclaimed Montessori method.

We believe every child is born with a natural drive to learn. Our prepared environment — rich with Montessori sensorial materials, practical life tools, and cultural wisdom — allows each child to discover, build, and flourish at their own pace. The result: children who are independent, empathetic, and genuinely in love with learning.

Our Philosophy

Why Choose
Little Gurukul?

Trust built on proven pedagogy, certified educators, and a deep love for childhood.

Book a School Tour

Certified Montessori Guides

Every educator is formally trained in Montessori pedagogy — not just caretakers, but true guides who respect the child's inner drive.

Safe & Hygienic Environment

Child-proofed spaces, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, non-toxic materials, and rigorous daily sanitization protocols for complete peace of mind.

Low Student–Teacher Ratio

A strict 1:8 ratio ensures every child receives individualized attention, enabling our guides to truly see and nurture each learner.

Whole-Child Development

Mindfulness corners, yoga, circle time, and cultural activities build emotional intelligence and social skills alongside academics.

Rooted in Indian Values

Gurukul wisdom — respect, discipline, curiosity — woven seamlessly with Montessori freedom. A uniquely Indian premium experience.

Nature-Rich Campus

Outdoor gardens, sandpit, climbing structures, and nature journaling bring children into joyful contact with the living world.

Premium Montessori Materials

Authentic, imported Montessori apparatus — pink tower, golden beads, moveable alphabet — that make abstract concepts beautifully concrete.

Parent as Partner

Regular parent–guide meetings, detailed progress portfolios, and open-door communication keep families genuinely involved in their child's journey.
